A student community for Anchor University

Campora is a place for Anchor University students to connect, share campus updates and discover what’s happening around them.

Share campus life

Publish posts and stories, join the discussion with comments and reactions, and follow the people whose updates you want to see.

Connect with other students

Search by display name to find a student. Follow someone to send a message request. Mutual follows unlock continued messaging.

Find events and opportunities

Check the campus calendar and published opportunities. Moderators manage listings and review reports from the community.

Join Campora

Create an account with your email, display name, username and password. You can use a personal email address.

Campora is currently available for Anchor University. Other universities are coming soon.
