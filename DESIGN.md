# Campora visual direction

Campus editorial: deep aubergine and clear violet ink on warm white paper.
The Campora mark and an abstract open-page illustration carry the identity.
Serif display type belongs to the welcome and feed masthead; interface text,
forms, lists and administration use sans serif. Use violet for the next action
and current destination, and borders to separate related information.

Dark mode uses deep plum for the canvas and panels, lavender for labels and
selection, and a brighter violet for primary actions. Form borders and
keyboard focus stand out from the surfaces around them.

Sign-in puts the email form first on small screens and pairs it with an
illustrated campus panel on desktop. The feed uses a masthead, a permanent
desktop navigation rail, and a calendar column. The workspace uses an
operations index and separate publishing, reporting and video sections.
